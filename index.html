Frequently Asked Questions
Why is my soil moisture reading drifting over time?
Capacitive soil sensors gradually accumulate mineral deposits at the probe surface, which alters the dielectric reading. Recalibrate every 4–6 weeks via Settings → Sensor Calibration. Wipe the probe clean with distilled water before measuring "dry" and "wet" reference values.
Can the system run if Wi-Fi goes down?
Yes — the Arduino performs all sensor polling and threshold-based actuation locally (edge computing). Wi-Fi is only required for the dashboard, time-lapse uploads, and MQTT telemetry. Schedules and automation continue normally offline; data is queued on the microSD and synced when Wi-Fi returns.
How do I add a second pod or greenhouse?
Each pod runs an independent Arduino + ESP32-CAM pair. Provision the new pair via Devices → "+ Add device", scan the QR code printed on the ESP32-CAM, and the dashboard will auto-discover its sensors and actuators. Multi-pod overview lands in v1.2.
What happens if the water reservoir runs dry?
If a pump cycle activates but soil moisture does not rise within 5 minutes, the system flags a "reservoir likely empty" warning, sounds the buzzer (if enabled), and emails you. The pump is throttled to one attempt per hour to prevent dry-running damage.
Is my data sent to any cloud service?
By default, telemetry stays on your local Wi-Fi network. Enabling MQTT in Settings publishes data to the configured broker (yours or our hosted one). Image captures stay on the microSD unless you opt in to cloud backup. No analytics or telemetry are collected without your consent.
How accurate are the temperature and humidity readings?
DHT22 spec: ±0.5°C and ±2–5% RH. For higher precision, swap to a SHT31 (±0.3°C, ±2% RH) — the firmware auto-detects the sensor on the I²C bus.
